Why Do We Need Sleep?

Sleep is an important part of staying healthy. It gives our bodies time to rest and helps our brains work properly. Getting enough sleep can improve our mood, memory, and concentration during the day.

Benefits of Sleep

Getting enough sleep helps us feel more awake and focused. It also supports the immune system and helps the body recover after a busy day.

Not getting enough sleep can make it harder to learn, remember information, and manage stress.

Stages of Sleep

Sleep has different stages. During non-REM sleep, the body relaxes and goes through several stages of deeper sleep. During REM sleep, the brain is active and most dreaming occurs.

These stages repeat throughout the night and help the body and brain get the rest they need.

Tips for Better Sleep

  • Go to bed at about the same time each night.
  • Keep your bedroom quiet and comfortable.
  • Avoid caffeine late in the day.
  • Try to put your phone away before bedtime.
  • Get regular exercise during the day.
