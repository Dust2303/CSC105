About the Team

Carlow University's Men's Soccer Program had its inaugural season in 2016 initially competting in the USCAA DI League. Over time the program has grown and evolved to the point where in 2022 the team was accepted into the NCAA Division III conference, and continues to compete in both USCAA DI and NCAA DIII conferences. The team prides itself on continued improvement over the past decade

Team Highlights

Currently the team has a 2-9 record. The team leaders in stats are as follows: Eric Fowler - 6 goals & 13 points Devan Donskoy - 4 assists, and Dustin Harrier - 84 saves & .743 SV%

Life on the Team

Playing for the Carlow University Men's Soccer Team is more than just sport, it's an outlet for student athletes balancing their academic and athletic commitments. Our players are dedicated to excellence both on and off the field, representing our university with pride and integrity.

Being part of the team goes farther than playing soccer, it builds character, making lifelong friends, connections, that allows for our players to have succesful careers in whatever field or profession they choose.
